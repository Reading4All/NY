*** Staff Counts & Salaries Notes
* Data Source - Personel Counts and Ave.Salaries: [NYSED Personnel Master File (PMF) Staff ](https://www.nysed.gov/information-reporting-services/personnel-master-file-pmf)
* Data Source - Student enrollment data: [ESSA Financial Reporting](https://www.nysed.gov/fiscal-analysis-research/essa-and-slfs-financial-reporting)
* "All - Administrators" = Central Administration + Program Administration
* "All Other - Professional Staff" = Nurses, Counselors, Psychologists, Social workers, Other non-teaching staff
* A district is omitted when that year's value is unavailable
* Salary change is each role's average salary (weighted by FTE) minus its average salary in the first year shown; a role with no first-year salary is omitted from the salary plot
* Charter schools and NYC districts are not included
